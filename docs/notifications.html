Set up
Notifications

Who hears what.

When someone changes a task's people, pull requests or status, Cade posts one message in the task's thread: what changed, then what it means for the people it names. There's nothing to confirm, and one change is one message, so everyone is notified once.

First, the change log

A line per change, who made it and what it was: “@ann assigned @rex, @bob as developer.” The thread keeps a log of every change.

Then, what it means for them

A line each: “@rex, @bob, could you pick this up when you get a chance? Thanks!” or “@tom, there's no need to test this anymore. Thanks!”

Only when there's something to do now

  • Reviewers are asked when a PR is linked, or when they're assigned and there are pending PRs (not yet sent for approval). They're asked about those PRs.
  • Testers are asked when the task moves to In testing, or when they're assigned and it's In testing already.
  • Developers are asked when they're assigned, unless the task is Done.
  • Those removed are told there's no need anymore only if they'd have been asked.

Assigning people

Developers, reviewers and testers

@Cade assign dev @rex

Thread
@ann assigned @rex as developer.
@rex, could you pick this up when you get a chance? Thanks!

A reviewer is asked about the pending PRs: “@mia, could you review web/12 when you get a chance? Thanks!” A tester is asked to “give this a test” once the task is In testing.

Assigned before there's anything to do

Thread
@ann assigned @mia as reviewer.
@ann assigned @tom as tester.

No pending PRs, and not In testing yet, so there's nothing to ask. Linking a PR asks the reviewers, and moving to In testing asks the testers.

Changing status

Asking testers

Moving to In testing, however it's done

Thread
@ann set the status to In testing.
@tom, could you give this a test when you get a chance? Thanks!

The same from @Cade status and its picker, the task card's Change status, suggestions and standup. A status typed in the command, like @Cade status testing, gets a ✅ on your message, with only the testers' request posted. The @Cade status picker becomes the change log; when testers are asked, it's replaced by a new reply instead, since Slack doesn't notify anyone about an edited message.
